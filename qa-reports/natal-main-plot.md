## Implemented
- `_shared/natalMainPlot.ts` selects structural candidates before the writer: traditional chart ruler, luminaries, angular houses, conjunctions to angles, tight personal contacts, angular/occupied house rulers, concentrations/configurations and repeated dispositor routes.
- Indicators are distinct source bodies, not repeated sign/house/condition rows. Opposite angles are one axis. Two structural indicators earn meaningful support; three earn main-story candidacy. Subset overlap prevents repeated versions of the same story. Candidate labels describe actual structure, not a canned psychological-theme list.
- Every candidate retains required evidence, complete ruler-path evidence, connected endpoint placements/contacts and competing needs. Contradictions are retained, not subtracted away.
- Map version 4 includes all ten planet chains, all angles, every available full house ruler chain, unrounded aspect orbs, canonical psychological function/sign/house context and the hierarchy. Complete tensions/reinforcements remain available.
- Server recomputes the hierarchy rather than trusting client ranking. Shared client/server validation requires selected theme IDs, the primary story, independent primary evidence in plain synthesis, exact per-conclusion aspect citations, relevant ruler paths and salient competing needs.
- Cache version 6 revalidates results. UI says Why astrologically, How this can show up and Integration; numeric support is not displayed. Gemini 3.1 Pro remains unchanged. No ephemeris/house math changes.
